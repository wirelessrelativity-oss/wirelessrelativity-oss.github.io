JOY IS GOD

Anchoring your heart in His eternal hope, grace, and truth.

Welcome to Joy Is God

True joy is not a temporary feeling tied to shifting circumstances—it is a steadfast, unshakeable gift rooted in the character of God and realized through a personal relationship with Jesus Christ. No matter where you are on your spiritual journey, you are welcome here.

"May the God of hope fill you with all joy and peace as you trust in him, so that you may overflow with hope by the power of the Holy Spirit."
— Romans 15:13

Promises of Hope, Peace & Strength

Explore timeless biblical truths that offer comfort during life's trials and deepen your connection with God:

"Peace I leave with you; my peace I give you. I do not give to you as the world gives. Do not let your hearts be troubled and do not be afraid."
— John 14:27
"Do not fear, for I am with you; do not be dismayed, for I am your God. I will strengthen you and help you; I will uphold you with my righteous right hand."
— Isaiah 41:10
"Cast all your anxiety on him because he cares for you."
— 1 Peter 5:7

Who is Jesus Christ?

Jesus is the central figure of human history and the foundation of the Christian faith. He is not merely a prophet or a good teacher; the Bible reveals Him as the Son of God, fully divine and fully human.

He is the Savior

Jesus came to earth with a specific mission: to seek and to save the lost. He willingly took the punishment for our sins upon Himself on the cross, offering us forgiveness and a pathway back to God.

He Conquered Death

The grave could not hold Him. Three days after His crucifixion, Jesus rose from the dead, proving His power over sin and death and offering eternal life to all who believe in Him.

"Jesus answered, 'I am the way and the truth and the life. No one comes to the Father except through me.'"
— John 14:6

The Character of God

To find true joy, we must know the One who gives it. The Bible teaches us several foundational truths about who God is:

  • God is Love: His love for you is unconditional and eternal. It does not depend on your performance, but on His perfect nature. (1 John 4:8)
  • God is Faithful: Even when circumstances change, God remains constant. His promises are trustworthy, and He will never abandon those who seek Him. (Lamentations 3:22-23)
  • God is Holy & Just: He is completely pure and righteous. Because He is just, He must judge sin, but because He is loving, He provided a substitute in Jesus Christ.

How to Accept Jesus Christ as Your Savior

God loves you deeply and desires a close, personal relationship with you. Because of human imperfection, we are separated from God, but His immense love bridged that gap through His Son, Jesus Christ. Here are the simple steps to receiving His free gift of eternal life:

  • Acknowledge your need: Recognize that we all fall short and need God's forgiveness (Romans 3:23).
  • Believe in Jesus: Understand that Jesus died on the cross for your sins and rose triumphantly from the dead (John 3:16).
  • Confess and Turn: Turn away from past burdens and invite Jesus to lead your life as Lord and Savior (Romans 10:9).

If you are ready to place your trust in Him, you can make this commitment right now with a sincere heart:

"Dear God, I know that I am a sinner, and I ask for your forgiveness. I believe that Jesus Christ died for my sins, was raised from the dead, and is alive today. I invite Jesus to come into my heart and take control of my life. Thank you for your grace, your gift of eternal salvation, and your unending joy. In Jesus' name, Amen."

If you prayed that prayer sincerely, the Bible says heaven is rejoicing over you! Welcome to the family of God.

Growing in Your Faith

Accepting Jesus is just the beginning of a beautiful journey. Here is how you can continue to grow in your walk with God:

  • Read the Bible: The Bible is God's living Word. Start by reading the Book of John in the New Testament to learn more about the life and teachings of Jesus.
  • Talk to God through Prayer: Prayer is simply having a conversation with God. You can talk to Him about your worries, your thanks, and your daily life at any time.
  • Find Community: Look for a local, Bible-believing church where you can worship, learn, and grow alongside other believers.

¿Quién es Jesucristo?

Jesús es la figura central de la historia humana y el fundamento de la fe cristiana. La Biblia lo revela como el Hijo de Dios, totalmente divino y totalmente humano.

Él es el Salvador

Jesús vino con una misión: buscar y salvar a los perdidos. Él tomó voluntariamente el castigo por nuestros pecados en la cruz.

Él Venció a la Muerte

Tres días después de su crucifixión, Jesús resucitó de entre los muertos, demostrando su poder sobre el pecado y ofreciendo vida eterna a todos los que creen en Él.

"Jesús le dijo: Yo soy el camino, y la verdad, y la vida; nadie viene al Padre, sino por mí."
— Juan 14:6

El Carácter de Dios

  • Dios es Amor: Su amor por ti es incondicional y eterno. (1 Juan 4:8)
  • Dios es Fiel: Sus promesas son confiables y nunca abandonará a quienes lo buscan. (Lamentaciones 3:22-23)
  • Dios es Santo y Justo: Él es completamente puro. Porque es justo, debe juzgar el pecado, pero porque es amoroso, proveyó un sustituto en Jesucristo.

Cómo Aceptar a Jesucristo

Debido a la imperfección humana, estamos separados de Dios, pero Su inmenso amor cerró esa brecha a través de Su Hijo. Aquí están los pasos para recibir la vida eterna:

  • Reconoce tu necesidad: Reconoce que necesitamos el perdón de Dios (Romanos 3:23).
  • Cree en Jesús: Comprende que Jesús murió en la cruz por tus pecados y resucitó (Juan 3:16).
  • Confiesa y vuélvete: Invita a Jesús a guiar tu vida como Señor y Salvador (Romanos 10:9).
"Dios querido, sé que soy un pecador, y te pido perdón. Creo que Jesucristo murió por mis pecados y resucitó. Invito a Jesús a entrar en mi corazón y tomar el control de mi vida. Amén."

Creciendo en tu Fe

  • Lee la Biblia: Comienza con el Evangelio de Juan para aprender más sobre Jesús.
  • Habla con Dios: La oración es simplemente tener una conversación con Él sobre tus preocupaciones y agradecimientos.
  • Encuentra Comunidad: Busca una iglesia local donde puedas adorar y crecer junto a otros creyentes.

盼望与平安的应许

"我留下平安给你们；我将我的平安赐给你们。我所赐的，不像世人所赐的。"
— 约翰福音 14:27

耶稣基督是谁？

耶稣是人类历史的核心人物，也是基督教信仰的根基。圣经启示祂是神的儿子，具有完全的神性和完全的人性。

祂是救主

耶稣来到世上是为了寻找和拯救失丧的人。祂甘愿在十字架上为我们的罪担当刑罚，赐予我们通向神的道路。

祂战胜了死亡

耶稣被钉十字架三天后从死里复活，证明了祂战胜罪恶和死亡的大能，并向所有信祂的人赐予永生。

神的属性

  • 神是爱： 祂对你的爱是无条件且永恒的。
  • 神是信实的： 即使环境改变，神依然不变，祂绝不离弃寻求祂的人。
  • 神是圣洁与公义的： 祂绝对纯洁。因着公义，祂必须审判罪；因着慈爱，祂借着耶稣基督为你预备了救赎。

如何接受耶稣基督

  • 承认你的需要： 认识到我们都需要神的赦免。
  • 相信耶稣： 明白耶稣为你的罪死在十字架上并复活。
  • 认罪并回转： 邀请耶稣作为你的生命之主。
"亲爱的神，我知道我是一个罪人，我向你寻求宽恕。我相信耶稣基督为我的罪死在十字架上并复活。我邀请耶稣进入我的心。奉耶稣的名祷告，阿们。"
