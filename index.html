Accept Jesus Christ & Be Born Again Today

No matter what burden, crisis, or trial you are facing today, Jesus Christ loves you and offers you immediate forgiveness, peace, and eternal life.

The Salvation Prayer: How to Accept Jesus Christ

Jesus said: "Most assuredly, I say to you, unless one is born again, he cannot see the kingdom of God." (John 3:3)

Understanding what it means to be saved is the first step toward spiritual freedom. Becoming born again means receiving a new, spiritual life through Jesus Christ. Your past is forgiven, your sins are washed away, and you become a child of God. If you want to accept Jesus as your personal Lord and Savior today, read and pray this simple prayer from your heart:

The Salvation Prayer:

"Lord Jesus Christ, I acknowledge that I am a sinner in need of Your grace. I believe with all my heart that You are the Son of God, that You died on the cross for my sins, and that You rose from the dead on the third day to give me eternal life.

Right now, I repent of my sins and open my heart to You. I ask You to forgive me, cleanse me, and come into my life. I confess Jesus Christ as my personal Lord and Savior. Thank You for saving me and making me born again today. Amen."

If you prayed that prayer sincerely, God's Word promises that you are saved! (Romans 10:9-10). You are now a brand-new creation in Christ.

Christian Comfort for Anxiety, Grief, and Crisis

Bible Verses and Prayers for Life's Challenges

Explore biblical encouragement and prayers tailored for your personal situation:

What Does It Mean to Be Born Again?

To be born again means experiencing a spiritual birth through faith in Jesus Christ. When you accept Him, your past sins are washed away, and the Holy Spirit grants you eternal spiritual life (2 Corinthians 5:17).

How to Pray for Peace of Mind and Comfort

When mental fatigue or heavy worry clouds your days, bring your thoughts to God in prayer. Scripture promises: "And the peace of God, which surpasses all understanding, will guard your hearts and minds through Christ Jesus." (Philippians 4:7).

Bible Verses for Overcoming Fear and Anxiety

Fear can feel overwhelming, but God gives us strength. 2 Timothy 1:7 reminds us: "For God has not given us a spirit of fear, but of power and of love and of a sound mind." (2 Timothy 1:7). Cast your worries onto Him daily.

Finding Christian Hope During Grief and Loss

Grief is a heavy burden, but you are not alone. Jesus promises comfort to those who mourn: "Blessed are those who mourn, for they shall be comforted." (Matthew 5:4).

Christian Salvation & Faith FAQs

How can I find peace and hope in difficult times?
True peace comes through a personal relationship with Jesus Christ, who promises rest and comfort to all who are weary, anxious, or facing trials. As Jesus says in John 14:27, "Peace I leave with you, My peace I give to you; not as the world gives do I give to you. Let not your heart be troubled, neither let it be afraid."
How can I deal with severe anxiety and worry?
You can find comfort by casting your anxieties on God through prayer. Trusting in His plan and reading scripture can bring supernatural peace to calm an anxious mind, as encouraged in 1 Peter 5:7: "casting all your care upon Him, for He cares for you."
Does God care about my physical health?
Yes, God cares deeply about your physical well-being. During health trials, praying for strength, seeking appropriate medical care, and leaning on His comforting presence can sustain you. 3 John 1:2 says, "Beloved, I pray that you may prosper in all things and be in health, just as your soul prospers."
